ResNet50 recipe × masking interaction pilot
==============================================

Development dataset:
CheXpert train/validation only.

Seed:
42.

Purpose:
Test whether the alternative full-image training recipe also improves
segmentation-guided Hard and Soft inputs.

Results (validation AUROC):
Original recipe:
  Full       0.756194267025
  Hard       0.740324189800
  Soft 0.20  0.747755253200

Alternative recipe:
  Full       0.750979666142
  Hard       0.731478910179
  Soft 0.20  0.751853908894

Alternative minus Original:
  Full      -0.005214600882
  Hard      -0.008845279621
  Soft 0.20 +0.004098655694

Within alternative recipe:
  Hard minus Full = -0.019500755963
  Soft minus Full = +0.000874242752

Interpretation:
This single-seed development-only pilot does not support a general
masking rescue from the alternative recipe. Hard masking remained
associated with lower validation discrimination. Soft attenuation was
approximately comparable to the full-image alternative model.

The experiment does not identify a causal contribution from augmentation,
initialization, learning rate, weight decay, batch size, scheduler, or any
other individual recipe component because the alternative recipe changes
multiple factors simultaneously.

No CheXpert test, RSNA, or PadChest data were used for this pilot.
